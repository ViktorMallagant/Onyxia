# Editorial review notes — not public lore

All entries in this release are taken from the supplied Onyxia documents. Original authorial wording and terminology have been preserved as much as possible; these items were not silently harmonised:

1. **The Dawn’s Light Vanguard:** The entry gives **Founded: 812**, while its narrative says it was designated as the Vanguard in **905**. These dates might describe different stages of the order, but the distinction is not made explicit.
2. **The economics document:** The expenditure percentages (35 + 30 + 10.5 + 10.5 + 10.5 + 5.5) add up to **102%**, not 100%. All percentages are displayed as written.
3. **Coin-weight example:** One Gold Daktari = 10,000 Copper Daktari is clear, but the stated copper tonnage does not appear consistent with one-ounce coins. The original statement is preserved.
4. **Chronology:** The home page describes the present as **Year 1400 of the Sun**, while the economics treatise lists balance sheets for **1401**. These lists are retained without assuming a new present year.
5. **Order name:** The organisation manuscript uses **St. Marcellie** and **St. Marceille** in different places. Both are preserved.
6. **Names and diacritics:** Spelling, realm names, and uncertain claims are reproduced from the provided manuscripts rather than normalised to external lore. The required Umbraeth spelling is respected; source variant **Umbraeths** remains a plural.

## Content and presentation
- 8 political realms and 2 special sites are separate cards.
- 17 sentient species appear in original emergence order.
- 16 bestiary creatures and the multiple images for Drooklins / Moonkins remain grouped.
- 8 magic types, 4 religious traditions, 5 organisations and 14 economics subsections are all included.
- All images are derived from the provided uploads and compressed to WebP for performance.
- Navigational and interface copy is in British English. This initial import does **not** perform a substantive rewrite of the source entries.
